# Zyne foundations

The design tokens behind every component: colors (light & dark), type, spacing, radii, shadows and motion. Components consume the **semantic aliases** documented here — never the raw primitives — so switching `data-theme` on `<html>` re-themes everything automatically.

## Colors

### Semantic aliases

Each row shows the light-theme and dark-theme value for the same alias. Contrast ratios are measured against `--bg-canvas` (or the paired surface, for `--text-on-accent`) and meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for UI borders) unless noted.

## Shadows

Light-theme shadows are tinted `rgba(55,47,55,…)`; dark-theme shadows are black plus a 1px white hairline at 4–6% opacity. Toggle the theme in the toolbar above to compare.

## Motion

Durations: **120ms** (fast), **180ms** (base), **260ms** (slow). Easing: `cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)` for entrances/state changes (`--ease-standard`), `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)` for exits (`--ease-exit`). Fades and rises move 4px or less; nothing scales on press. Every transition respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` by dropping to 0ms.
